Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 2, 2026  ·  Version 1.1.0

This Privacy Policy describes how Link Cleaner (the “Extension”) handles information when you use it. We believe in transparency and minimal data collection — this document explains precisely what we handle, why we handle it, and the choices available to you.

Overview Information Features Permissions Sharing Retention Security Children Changes Contact

1. Overview

Link Cleaner is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. The Extension does not require you to create an account, does not sell personal information, and does not share your information with third parties for advertising purposes.

We collect only what is necessary to provide the features you request, and we retain it only for as long as needed to deliver those features.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use the Extension, the following categories may be involved:

The Extension does not use external analytics that identifies you, and does not create a cross-site advertising profile.

3. How the Extension Works

Link Cleaning

When you request to clean a URL, the Extension processes the address within your browser to remove known tracking and affiliate parameters and to present a normalized version suitable for sharing.

Shortened Links

When you choose to clean a shortened URL (for example, t.co, bit.ly, or amzn.to), the Extension resolves the destination address to present the cleaned final URL. This resolution is performed only after you explicitly request it.

QR Codes

When you choose to generate a QR code for a cleaned link, the cleaned URL is transmitted to a third-party QR image service for the sole purpose of creating the image. This transmission occurs only when you open the QR view.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing or advertising purposes.

Information is disclosed only as necessary to deliver the features described in Section 3, and only as a direct result of an action you take. For example, a shortened URL is resolved only when you choose to clean it, and a QR image is generated only when you open that view.

Compliance: Our use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used solely to provide or improve user-facing features of the Extension, is not used for advertising or unrelated purposes, and is not shared with third parties except as described in Section 3.

6. Data Retention and Your Choices

  • History: You may clear your cleaning history at any time via History → Clear in the Extension. This permanently removes those entries from your browser.
  • Preferences: You may modify or reset your preferences at any time through the Extension’s menu or settings page.
  • Uninstalling: Removing the Extension removes associated data from your browser’s extension storage.
  • Support: If you contact us and voluntarily provide information, we use it solely to respond to your inquiry and do not retain it longer than necessary.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information handled by the Extension. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We encourage you to keep your browser and operating system up to date.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Extension is a general-purpose browser tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address the matter.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Extension, legal requirements, or our practices. When we do, we will revise the “Last Updated” date above and, where appropriate, note material changes in the Chrome Web Store listing. Continued use of the Extension after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
